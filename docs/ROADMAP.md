# Roadmap

## v0.1 — vertical slice (DONE)
Island generation with guarantees, turn system, items/inventory/gathering,
crafting, building, survival meters, food/water/spoilage, day/night, weather,
creatures + combat, POIs with loot, raft + island travel, save/load, UI,
headless tests.

## v0.2 — deeper survival
- Injury consequences: infection, fractures, bandage/medkit tiers
- Sleep quality, night cold vs shelter tiers
- Cooking station, water boiling without bottle limits
- Skin/fur clothing from boars, warmth layering

## v0.3 — expanded archipelago
- More islands, seeded variety (atolls, twin islands, reefs)
- Inter-island swimming hazards, currents
- Fog-of-war on world map with partial coastline hints

## v0.4 — boats
- Modular raft: floats/deck/sail/rudder/motor/fuel tank with speed & capacity
- Real sailing legs: day-by-day voyages, storms damaging the boat
- Shipwreck salvage → boat upgrades

## v0.5 — NPCs
- Friendly survivors: trade, recruit, camp quests
- Faction reputation for hostile camps

## v0.6 — settlements
- Player base building tiers, storage networks, farms
- Fishing traps / rain collectors automation (chore removal)

## v0.7 — advanced simulation
- Temperature model per clothing/wetness
- Illness system (dirty water, food poisoning, infection)
- Weather fronts with wind direction affecting sailing

## v0.8 — content expansion
- 100+ items, ranged weapons (bow → pipe rifle), ammo crafting
- Marine life, bird flocks, island fauna per biome

## v0.9 — polish/balance
- Audio pass, animation pass, shader water/lighting
- Balance sweep from telemetry of test runs

## v1.0 — release-quality core
- Full vertical loop: survive → build boat → crew → multi-island story nodes
- Steam Deck verified defaults, graphical options
